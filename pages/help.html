FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I issue a book to a member?

Open the Book Catalog, select the required book, and use the issue option to record the transaction.

How do I return an issued book?

Open the issue and return section, locate the member's transaction, and update the book status when it is returned.

How can I manage library members?

Open Members from the main menu to view and manage student and library member records.

Where can I manage reservations?

Open Reservations from the main menu to view and manage active, pending, and completed reservations.

Still need help?

Contact the system administrator or college library support desk for further assistance.
